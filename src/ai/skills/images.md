# Images

## Pick the right model
- **Fast model:** quick suggestions where the image barely affects the final output: storyline
  previews, style options, layout ideas.
- **Quality model:** anything that ends up in the Flick: thumbnails, images on the canvas.
- **Image to image:** whenever the user's images should be kept (their product, character or
  style). Pass them as references and describe only what changes.

## Using the user's images
When the prompt and the user's images match, use them as references. Infer what the images are
for; confirm when in doubt.

## Thumbnails
- One per variation. Generate them to match the variations.
- Easy to tell apart at a glance.
- 1:1, the product centred, with the same pose and placement in every thumbnail.
- No text unless it's needed.
- Look at each thumbnail after it's made; remake any that doesn't match the set.
