# Video prompts

The reference video sets the stage; every variation is made from it. Consistency is everything.

## Reference prompt: 1 to 3 sentences, in this order
1. **Subject**: the product (and person, if any), described concretely: material, colour, shape.
2. **Action**: one simple motion: a slow push-in, a gentle orbit, a hand lifting it, a walk past.
3. **Camera**: shot size and move: "close-up, slow push-in", "medium shot, slow orbit left".
4. **Setting and light**: one location, one light: "sunlit concrete wall, golden hour",
   "seamless studio backdrop, soft top light".
5. **Style**: "cinematic, shallow depth of field", "clean commercial look".

Example: "Close-up of matte black sport sunglasses on an athlete against a sunlit concrete wall,
slow push-in, golden hour light, cinematic, shallow depth of field."

## Variation prompts: say only what changes
Write "Same scene, <the change>." and nothing else that could drift:
- "Same scene, gold mirrored lenses."
- "Same scene, the model wears the rust tiered gown."
Never re-describe the camera, light or setting; never add new actions.

## Avoid (video models get these wrong)
- Readable text, logos or price tags inside the video (put text on the canvas instead).
- Hands doing fine detail, many people, fast cuts, multiple scenes, crowds.
- Brand names of other companies; real celebrities.
- Vague words alone ("beautiful", "amazing"); be concrete.

## Format
- Vertical 9:16: subject centred, room top and bottom for text.
- Square 1:1 and display sizes: subject centred, simple background.
- Horizontal 16:9: subject off-centre, leave a side for text.
