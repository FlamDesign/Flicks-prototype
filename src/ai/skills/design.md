# Design

## Hierarchy (top to bottom in a stack)
label (small caps, optional) → headline (big) → subheading (optional) → price → button.
One stack only. Keep it in one place: the lower third (default), the top, or the centre for
statement pieces. The thumbnail strip sits along the bottom (or a side) and nothing covers it.

## By format
| Format | Headline | Extras | Strip |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1080×1920 (9:16) | ≤ 6 words | label, price, button | bottom, medium |
| 1080×1080 (1:1) | ≤ 5 words | price, button | bottom, medium or small |
| 1920×1080 (16:9) | ≤ 6 words, left aligned | price, button | right side or bottom, small |
| 300×600, 320×480 | ≤ 4 words | price, button | bottom, small |
| 300×250, 336×280, 250×250, 480×320 | ≤ 3 words | button only (price if short) | bottom, small |
Small display sizes: no subheading, no label, at most 2 lines of text in total.

## Safe zones
- Keep text 5% in from every edge; 8% on 9:16 top and bottom.
- Never over the product or a face: put the stack where the video is empty.

## Colour and contrast
- Text on video: white (or the brand's light colour) on darker footage, near-black on light footage.
- The button is the brand's primary colour with white or black text, whichever contrasts more.
- At most 2 colours plus white/black. Use the brand's colours when known.

## Type
- One font family (the brand's, or DM Sans / Inter for clean, Montserrat for bold, Playfair Display for luxury).
- Headline bold; everything else regular.

## Button styles
pill (friendly, default), sharp (fashion, luxury), rounded (neutral), light (on dark footage),
outline (minimal), glass (premium, over busy footage).

## Thumbnail strip
- Square thumbnails; rounded for most, circle for beauty and food, square for fashion and tech.
- Highlight ring in the brand colour or white.
- Same size as other UI: medium on 9:16 and 1:1, small on display sizes.
