# Design

## Elements
- **Ads:** always a headline and a button (call to action). Add a label, price or subheading only
  when they help the decision.
- **Links and embeds:** decide what helps, suggest it, and ask the user.
- The visual design and layout are **identical across variations.** Words, prices, links and button
  text may differ per variation (feeds).
- Don't change anything the user didn't ask for, unless the prompt requires it.

## Brand first
1. Follow the brand's guidelines (fonts, colours, logo rules, tone).
2. Not given, but a brand you know: look up its guidelines.
3. No brand info: choose what's best for the agreed style, aiming for the best visual design overall.

## Hierarchy: a short story, read in 2 seconds
Who (logo or label) → what and why (headline) → how to act (button).
- **One focal point:** the swap in the video. Text supports it, never competes.
- **Headline:** the biggest text; one message.
- **Button:** the most obvious action; placed after the headline in reading order (below it, or
  to its right).
- **Total copy:** about 5 to 10 words on screen, plus the button.

## Layout rules
- **One block of text,** aligned to one edge (left) or centred. Never scattered.
- **Safe margins:** text at least 5% in from every edge; 8% at the top and bottom of 9:16.
- **Never over the subject:** place text where the video is calm and empty, never over the product
  or faces (the shots skill keeps those areas clear).
- **Spacing:** one rhythm (for example 8, 16, 24); more space around the button than between lines.
- **The thumbnail strip** is never covered: along the bottom or one side, nothing else on it.
- **Never overlap, never crop, always easy to see.** Check after every change.

## Type
- One font family, at most two weights: headline bold, everything else regular.
- Readable sizes: on display ads the headline is at least 16 px; supporting text and disclaimers
  can be smaller but stay legible.
- Sentence case for headlines. No full stop unless it's a statement for effect.

## Colour and contrast
- Brand colours plus white or black; at most two accent colours.
- **Text on video:** white (or the brand's light colour) on darker footage, near-black on light
  footage. Aim for a contrast ratio of 4.5:1 for normal text and 3:1 for large text and the button
  against the video. If the footage is busy, add a soft shade behind the text block.
- **Button:** the brand's main colour, with text in white or black (whichever contrasts more).

## Button styles
| Style | Suits |
|---|---|
| pill | friendly, general (default) |
| sharp | fashion, luxury, editorial |
| rounded | neutral |
| light | dark footage |
| outline | minimal brands |
| glass | premium, over busy footage |

## By size
| Size | Text block | Headline | Button | Thumbnail strip |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1080×1920 (9:16) | lower third, or the top | up to 6 words | under the headline | bottom, medium |
| 1080×1080 (1:1) | lower third | up to 5 words | under the headline | bottom or right |
| 1920×1080 (16:9) | left third, left aligned | up to 6 words | under the headline | right side or bottom, small |
| 300×600, 320×480 | top or bottom | up to 4 words | bottom | bottom, small |
| 300×250, 336×280, 250×250, 480×320 | one line, top or bottom | up to 3 words | bottom right | bottom, small; no subheading or label |

## Copy
- Short, as in ads. **Headline:** a benefit or promise, 2 to 6 words ("Find your lens",
  "Built for wet days"). **Button:** a verb, 1 to 3 words ("Shop now", "Get yours"); name the
  variation when it differs ("Shop Gold").
- **Variation names:** short and clear, one word preferred, at most 14 characters.
- **Prices:** exact, with currency; never invented.
- **Category lines** when they apply: alcohol, "Please drink responsibly."; no health, finance or
  "best in the world" claims unless the user gives them.
- Nothing is banned outright, but nothing may harm the brand's reputation, or Flicks'.
