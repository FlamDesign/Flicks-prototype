# Design

## Elements
- **Ads:** always a headline and a call-to-action button. Add a label, price or subheading only
  when they help.
- **Links and embeds:** decide what helps, suggest it, and ask the user.
- The visual design and layout are identical across variations. Words, prices, links and button
  text may differ per variation.
- Don't change anything the user didn't ask for, unless the prompt requires it.

## Brand first
- Follow the brand's guidelines first. If they aren't given but you know the brand, look them up.
- No brand info: choose what's best for the agreed style, and aim for the best visual design overall.

## Layout principles
- **Hierarchy:** one focal point. Order: label, headline, subheading, price, button. The headline
  is the biggest text; the button is the most obvious action.
- **One block of text,** aligned to one edge or centred, never scattered.
- **Safe margins:** keep text about 5% in from every edge (8% at the top and bottom of 9:16).
- **Never over the subject:** place text where the video is calm and empty; never over the
  product or faces.
- **Contrast:** text must stand out from the video behind it (aim for 4.5:1). Use white on dark
  footage, near-black on light footage. Button text must contrast with the button.
- **Few colours:** brand colours plus white or black; at most two accent colours.
- **Type:** one font family, at most two weights. Headline bold, everything else regular.
- **Spacing:** consistent gaps on one rhythm (for example 8, 16, 24); more space around the
  button than between text lines.
- **Thumbnail strip:** never covered; along the bottom or one side, with nothing else on it.
- Never overlap, never crop, always easy to see.

## By size
| Size | Text | Thumbnail strip |
|---|---|---|
| 1080×1920 (9:16) | lower third or top; headline up to 6 words | bottom |
| 1080×1080 (1:1) | lower third; headline up to 5 words | bottom or right |
| 1920×1080 (16:9) | left third; headline up to 6 words | right side or bottom |
| 300×600, 320×480 | top or bottom; headline up to 4 words | bottom, small |
| 300×250, 336×280, 250×250, 480×320 | headline up to 3 words and the button only | bottom, small |

## Copy
- Short, as in ads. Headline: a benefit, 2 to 6 words. Button: a verb, 1 to 3 words.
- Variation names: short and clear; one word preferred.
- Nothing is banned outright, but nothing may harm the brand's reputation, or Flicks'.
