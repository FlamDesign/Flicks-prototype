# Formats

## Sizes by placement (what the size question offers)
| Where it runs | Sizes |
|---|---|
| In ads | Medium rectangle 300×250, Large rectangle 336×280, Square 250×250, Mobile interstitial 320×480, Mobile interstitial landscape 480×320, Half page 300×600, Square 1:1 1080×1080, Horizontal 16:9 1920×1080, Vertical 9:16 1080×1920 |
| On a website or app banner | Ultra wide 21:9 2520×1080, Wide 16:9 1920×1080, Landscape 4:3 1440×1080, Square 1:1 1080×1080, Portrait 3:4 1080×1440, Tall 9:16 1080×1920 |
| As a link | Square 1:1 1080×1080, Wide 16:9 1920×1080, Tall 9:16 1080×1920 |
Any width×height can be typed. Videos are made in the closest shape (21:9, 16:9, 4:3, 1:1, 3:4,
9:16) and cover the frame, centered: an unusual size (728×90) crops the edges, so keep the subject
centered. Length 4 to 15 s, default 8. Don't quote a resolution.

## Channel words
ad, ads, Meta, Google ads, display → In ads. Instagram, Reels, Stories, TikTok, Shorts → In ads,
9:16 (1:1 for "feed"). YouTube → In ads, 16:9 (Shorts 9:16). banner, site, landing page, hero,
homepage → On a website or app banner. link, QR, WhatsApp, email, share → As a link. square,
vertical, portrait, landscape, or numbers → that size.

## Clear zones (the swap and faces stay out of them)
| Size | Keep clear |
|---|---|
| 9:16 | the bottom third (button and strip) and the top 8% |
| 1:1 | the bottom quarter |
| 16:9 | the left third (text) and a strip along the bottom or right |
| 300×600, 320×480 | the bottom quarter |
| small display sizes | the bottom third; subject in the top two thirds |

## Layout per size (starting points)
| Size | Text block | Headline | Button | Thumbnail strip |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 9:16 | lower third, or the top | up to 6 words | under the headline | bottom, medium |
| 1:1 | lower third | up to 5 words | under the headline | bottom or right |
| 16:9 | left third, left aligned | up to 6 words | under the headline | right side or bottom, small |
| 300×600, 320×480 | top or bottom | up to 4 words | bottom | bottom, small |
| 300×250, 336×280, 250×250, 480×320 | one line, top or bottom | up to 3 words | bottom right | bottom, small; no subheading or label |
Safe margins: text at least 5% from every edge, 8% top and bottom on 9:16. The strip is never covered.
