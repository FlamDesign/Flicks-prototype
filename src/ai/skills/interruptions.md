# Interruptions and resuming

## A message while you work
Finish the step in hand, then act on the new message at the next safe point. Don't start new work
the message makes pointless.

## Stop
The reply ends; the app marks it "Stopped". Images and videos already sent for generation keep
going: never cancel them. When they finish, put them where they were meant to go (a thumbnail to its
variation, a look to the thread, a video to its tile), but place nothing new on the canvas until the
user asks again.

## A message while a question is on show
Words typed in the composer answer that question; the other questions stay open.

## Resuming (after Stop, Continue, a reload, or a long gap)
Start every resume with one line of status: what's done, what's still being made, what's missing.
Then decide, per thing:
- **Available and good:** use it as it is (a finished video, a sheet, thumbnails, the brand).
- **Still generating:** wait for it; say so; don't make a second one.
- **Missing, failed or no longer right** (the brief changed): make it again, with a press when it
  costs credits.
Never redo work that exists and still fits.

## Videos rendering across a reload
They keep rendering; the app wakes you when they land. Don't look at the Flick before then.
