# Concepts

## Offer concepts
- Suggest up to 3 concepts. Each one: a one-line idea, what changes between variations, the
  variations (2 to 5, default 3), and the story in a few beats.
- Show each storyline with quick images (the fast image model) so the user sees the style of the
  video before anything is generated. These images are for direction, not final frames.
- Let the user pick, mix or change; confirm before moving on.

## Choose what changes
- Anything that makes sense can change: product, colour, flavour, person, outfit, scene, time of
  day, season, offer. Suggest what could change; confirm with the user.
- Change the minimum needed, but change everything that belongs together. Example: an ice-cream
  video where the flavour changes also changes the packaging, the cream colour and the fruit
  floating around it.
- If a request looks odd, flag it and suggest a better option; don't block it.
- Whatever changes must be on screen at least 40 to 50% of the time, and big enough to see easily.

## What usually changes, by category
| Category | Usually changes | Strong video types | Watch out for |
|---|---|---|---|
| Drinks | flavour, can or bottle colour, serve, occasion | pour, condensation close-up, hand-to-camera, bar or picnic moment | hands covering the label, liquid splashing over the product |
| Food and quick service | menu item, topping, meal size | hero rotate, steam close-up, top-down spread | bites hiding the food, messy plates |
| Beauty and skincare | shade, product, model, finish | face close-up, texture swatch, product on a plinth | hands across the face, harsh light on skin |
| Fashion | outfit, colourway, model | full-body pose, walk to camera, garment detail | twirls, flowing fabric, fast walking |
| Footwear | colourway, model | ground-level step, close-up on material, on-foot walk | fast running, feet crossing |
| Tech | finish, model, accessory | slow orbit hero, in-hand, detail on edges and ports | readable screens and interface text |
| Automotive | paint colour, trim, setting | front three-quarter hero, rolling shot, drone follow | fast pass-bys, road-level blur |
| Travel and hotels | destination, room, time of day, season | aerial establish, person looking out, slow reveal | crowds, animals, choppy water near people |
| Finance and apps | persona, life moment, benefit | person with phone or card, reaction close-up | readable numbers or screens |
| Luxury, jewellery, watches | metal, stone, strap, model | macro facets, slow orbit on velvet, on wrist or neck | sparkle changing between variations |
| Home and appliances | finish, palette, room style | room wide shot, product in use, detail push-in | moving pets or people in the background |
| Packaged goods | flavour, variant, pack size | pack hero, ingredients arrangement, in-hand | ingredients flying across the pack |
| Sports and fitness | athlete, gear colourway, sport | low-angle athlete, effort close-up, gear hero | fast action, shaky camera |
| Gaming and entertainment | character, world, mode | screen-glow face, neon set, device close-up | readable screens and logos |
| Kids and family | toy, character, setting | play at child height, toy hero, family moment | fast-moving children, many people |

If a brief fits no category, say so, and use the closest one for defaults.
