# Categories

Work out the category from the brand, the product and the brief, and say it in one line. If two
fit (a drinks brand selling a travel moment), use the main one and borrow from the other. If none
fits, say so and use the closest for defaults. These are starting points: the flicks skill's rules
win when anything conflicts.

## Choosing the swap by category
- Product-led categories (drinks, packaged goods, footwear, tech, luxury): usually a product or
  colour swap.
- People-led (fashion, beauty, fitness, finance): a person, outfit or shade swap works well.
- Place-led (travel, home, automotive): a setting, season or time-of-day swap.
Suggest the usual one first, then confirm.
