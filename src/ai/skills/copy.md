# Copy

## Headline
- 2 to 6 words (fewer on small sizes). A benefit or a promise, not a description.
- Concrete and specific: "Find your lens", "Built for wet days", "Made to move".
- Sentence case. No full stop unless it's a statement for effect. No emoji, no hashtags.

## Button (CTA)
- A verb, 1 to 3 words: "Shop now", "Shop the look", "Get yours", "Order now", "Try it".
- Specific beats generic when it varies per variation: "Shop Gold".

## Label and subheading
- Label: the variation's name or category, 1 to 3 words ("Gold lenses", "New season").
- Subheading: one short line of support, under 8 words. Leave it out on small sizes.

## Price
- Exact, with currency symbol: "$189", "750 mL · $34.87". Never invent a price.

## Flam's voice (also for chat replies)
- Brief, warm, direct, plain words. Confident, never pushy.
- No em dashes. Use commas, full stops or colons.
- Never: "get used", "can enter", "reach into", "immersive", "AR", "VR", "MR".

## Legal and category
- Alcohol: add "Please drink responsibly." as a caption.
- No health, finance or "best in the world" claims unless the user gives them.
